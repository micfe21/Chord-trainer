🎵 Chord Trainer – User Manual

Practice Tool for Musicians

What is this app?

This is a practice tool for musicians who want to get better at recognising and playing chords instantly. Instead of just showing you chords on a page, it:

  • Shows you a chord symbol (like F#m7 or V7)
  • You play it on a real MIDI keyboard or the on‑screen piano
  • It checks if you played it correctly
  • Then moves on to the next chord

The goal is to train your brain to translate a chord symbol into the right notes without thinking — exactly what you need for jazz, accompanying, or sight‑reading.

Quick Start (5 seconds)

  1. Open the app in your browser.
  2. Tap "Start Audio" (orange button) – this turns on the sound.
  3. Tap "Generate" – this creates a chord sequence.
  4. Read the chord shown in the big display (e.g., V7).
  5. Play it on your keyboard or the on‑screen piano.
  6. Get feedback – green = correct, red/blue = wrong.
  7. Repeat – it automatically moves to the next chord.

That's it. You're practising.

The Main Screen – What's What

1. The Big Chord Display

  • Shows the chord you need to play right now.
  • The badge (e.g., Roman) tells you what mode you're in.
  • If Strict Inversions is on, you'll also see the inversion (e.g., (1st inv.)).

2. The Progress Indicator

  • Shows 2 / 6 – you're on chord 2 of 6.
  • Turns green when you finish the whole sequence.

3. The On‑Screen Keyboard

  • Two octaves of piano keys.
  • Tap them with your mouse or finger.
  • They light up when you play (green = correct, red = wrong, blue = missing).

4. The Sequence Bar

  • Shows all the chords in your current sequence.
  • The current chord is highlighted in gold.
  • Tap any chord to hear it.

5. The Controls (top row)

6. The Filter Rows

Filter Row (All / Diatonic / Extended / Altered / Sus / Aug / °7 / Slash)

  • Limits which types of chords appear in the sequence.

Practice Library Row (Major / Minor / Dominant 7 / Major 7 / ...)

  • Limits which families of chords appear.
  • Great for drilling a specific chord type.

Borrowed Chords

  • When "On", the generator occasionally uses chords from other keys (e.g., ♭VII).
  • This makes progressions more interesting and trains you to handle unexpected chords.

7. The MIDI Button

  • Tap to connect your Bluetooth or USB MIDI keyboard.
  • If you don't have one, use the on‑screen keyboard instead.

8. The Sound Toggle

  • Internal – the app makes sound (warm piano‑like tone with reverb).
  • MIDI Thru – the app mutes its own sound so you can use an external synth (like Pianoteq) while still getting chord feedback.

9. The Action Buttons (bottom row)

10. The Stats Row

  • ✅ Correct – number of chords you got right.
  • ❌ Wrong – number of chords you got wrong.
  • 🔥 Streak – your current consecutive correct streak.
  • ⚡ Time – your average response time in milliseconds.

How to Practise

Basic Practice

  1. Tap Generate.
  2. Read the chord symbol.
  3. Play it on your keyboard or the on‑screen piano.
  4. The app tells you if you're right or wrong.
  5. Repeat until the sequence is complete.

Focused Practice (Practice Library)

If you want to drill a specific chord type:

  1. Tap one of the library buttons (e.g., "Dominant 7").
  2. Tap Generate.
  3. Now only dominant 7th chords will appear.
  4. Perfect for mastering a weak spot.

Voice Leading Practice

  1. Tap the "Voice Leading" button (it turns green and shows ✓).
  2. Tap Generate.
  3. Each chord will be inverted so your hand moves as little as possible between chords.
  4. This is excellent for practising smooth, musical voice‑leading.

Strict Inversion Practice

  1. Tap the "Strict Inversions" button (it turns red and shows ✓).
  2. Tap Generate.
  3. Each chord will now show its inversion (e.g., (1st inv.)).
  4. You must play the exact inversion shown – if you play the right notes but in a different inversion, the app will tell you.
  5. This builds precise finger memory and is essential for jazz and classical voicing practice.

Sight‑Reading Preparation

  1. Set Length to 10 or more.
  2. Disable Borrowed Chords (so the key stays stable).
  3. Try to play each chord as quickly as possible.
  4. The auto‑check will move you forward when you're correct.
  5. Increase speed by trying to play before the auto‑check triggers.

The Feedback System – What the Colours Mean

ColourMeaning
GreenYou played a note that belongs in the chord.
RedYou played a note that doesn't belong in the chord (extra note).
BlueA note that belongs in the chord hasn't been played yet (missing note).

The feedback clears after 1.5 seconds so you can try again.

Tips for Best Experience

On an iPad / Tablet

  • Rotate to landscape for the best keyboard size.
  • Use Web MIDI Browser (free from the App Store) for Bluetooth MIDI.
  • Tap "Start Audio" first – this is required on iOS.

On a Desktop (Windows / Mac)

  • Use Chrome or Edge for the best Web MIDI support.
  • Connect your keyboard via USB for the most reliable connection.

If you hear no sound

  • Tap "Start Audio" (orange button).
  • Make sure Sound is set to "Internal" (not "MIDI Thru").
  • Check your device volume.

If you get a "No notes played" message

  • Hold the notes for at least 500ms – the auto‑check needs a moment.
  • Or tap "Check" manually.

What the App Does Not Do

  • ❌ It doesn't teach you music theory from scratch.
  • ❌ It doesn't show you the answer on the keyboard before you play (that would defeat the purpose).
  • ❌ It doesn't record your progress across sessions (yet – coming soon).

Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
No soundTap "Start Audio". Make sure volume is up.
MIDI not connectingTap the MIDI button. If your device appears, select it. If not, double‑tap the MIDI status text to refresh.
On‑screen keyboard doesn't workTry tapping the keys. If nothing happens, tap "Start Audio" first.
Chords don't advancePlay the chord and hold it for a moment. Auto‑check needs about 500ms.
App feels slowTry a shorter sequence (4 or 6 chords).
Too much reverbThat can be adjusted — contact the developer.
